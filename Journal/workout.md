
# MMA Foundation Program — Beginner (5 Days/Week: Mon–Fri, Sat & Sun Off)

**Goal:** Build strength, explosive power, and core first — before adding dedicated cardio work. Legs are trained specifically on Friday. Fix "skinny-fat" body composition by building real muscle.

**Important note:** This is your **strength & conditioning (S&C)** program — the physical base. You'll still need to join a boxing/Muay Thai/BJJ/wrestling gym separately to learn actual technique. S&C + skill training together = MMA fighter.

**Why less cardio for now:** Building a strength/power/muscle base first, with technique-gym training providing your cardio stimulus naturally (through sparring, drilling, pad work), is a smart sequencing choice for a beginner — you'll add dedicated conditioning back in once your strength foundation and muscle are further along.

## On Being "Skinny Fat" (Body Composition Notes)
Skinny-fat usually means low muscle mass + higher body fat %, not just a weight problem. The fix is mainly **building muscle** (which this program does) plus a few nutrition basics:

- **Protein:** aim for roughly 1.6–2g per kg of bodyweight per day (chicken, eggs, fish, dairy, legumes) — this is what actually builds the muscle that changes your shape.
- **Don't crash-diet:** a hard calorie deficit while trying to build muscle will keep you "skinny" without fixing the "fat" — a slight deficit or maintenance calories with high protein works better while you're new to training.
- **Sleep 7–9 hours:** matters as much as training for body composition and recovery.

## Progression Rules (Beginner)
- **Weeks 1–2:** Focus on form, use light-moderate weight. Don't chase heavy numbers yet.
- **Weeks 3–4:** Add weight once you can complete all sets/reps with good form.
- **Every 4 weeks:** Reassess — increase weight, reps, or reduce rest slightly.
- If a movement causes joint pain (not muscle fatigue), stop and swap it — flag it and I'll adjust.

## A Few Honest Reminders
- This plan builds your **base**. Actual "MMA fighter" skills (striking technique, wrestling, submissions) come from a coach and mat time — S&C supports that, it doesn't replace it.
- Expect soreness in week 1–2 — that's normal adaptation, not injury, as long as it fades within a few days.
- Once your strength/power base and technique training are underway, we can layer dedicated conditioning back in (probably replacing one power day, or adding a 6th day) — just let me know when you're ready for that.
