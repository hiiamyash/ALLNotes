# 6-Day Lean Build Workout Plan

**Level:** Beginner (Week 1) | **Goal:** Lean, toned muscle | **Access:** Full commercial gym **Schedule:** Mon–Sat train, Friday = Legs, Sunday = Rest

---

## How to use this plan (read this first)

- **Rep ranges 10–15** for most exercises → this builds muscle _without_ excess bulk, which fits a lean-build goal. Heavier compound lifts (squat, deadlift, bench, press) sit at 8–10 for strength-base.
- **Rest:** 60–90 sec between sets on isolation moves, 90–120 sec on heavy compounds.
- **Progressive overload:** Add a little weight or 1 rep every week or two — this is the actual driver of results, not just "showing up."
- **Since you're one week in:** prioritize learning the _movement pattern_ with lighter weight before chasing load. Ego lifting now is the #1 cause of stalled beginner progress and injury.
- **Lean build also depends on nutrition:** moderate calorie deficit or maintenance, high protein (0.8–1g per lb bodyweight), and 2–3 short cardio sessions/week (20 min incline walk or cycling) will do more for "lean" than any exercise choice.

## DAY 1 – Monday: Chest & Triceps

### 1. Barbell Bench Press — 4 sets x 8-10

- **Pro tip:** Drive your feet into the floor and squeeze your shoulder blades together before unracking — this creates a stable base and protects your shoulders.
- **Do:** Lower the bar to mid-chest with control (2-3 sec).
- **Don't:** Bounce the bar off your chest or flare elbows to 90° — this strains the shoulder joint and reduces chest activation.

### 2. Incline Dumbbell Press — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Set the bench to 30°, not 45° — anything steeper shifts work to front delts instead of upper chest.
- **Do:** Press the dumbbells slightly inward at the top to squeeze the chest.
- **Don't:** Let the dumbbells drop too low behind the shoulder line — it overstretches the shoulder capsule.

### 3. Cable Fly (or Pec Deck) — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Think "hugging a tree" — keep a slight bend in the elbow throughout, don't turn it into a press.
- **Do:** Squeeze and hold for 1 sec at full contraction.
- **Don't:** Use so much weight that your shoulders roll forward — this is a finishing/isolation move, not a max-effort lift.

### 4. Tricep Rope Pushdown — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Keep elbows pinned to your sides the entire set — if they drift forward, you're using shoulders, not triceps.
- **Do:** Split the rope apart at the bottom for a full contraction.
- **Don't:** Lean your whole body weight into the pull — isolate the arms only.

### 5. Overhead Dumbbell Tricep Extension — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Keep your elbows close to your ears, not flared out, to keep tension on the triceps.
- **Don't:** Go too heavy — this is a common exercise for elbow pain when ego lifted.
- **Do:** Lower until you feel a stretch, not pain, behind the arm.

## DAY 2 – Tuesday: Back & Biceps

### 1. Lat Pulldown — 4 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Pull the bar to your upper chest while thinking "elbows to back pockets" — this maximizes lat engagement over bicep involvement.
- **Do:** Lean back slightly (10-15°) and control the weight on the way up.
- **Don't:** Yank the bar down using body momentum or pull behind the neck — both increase injury risk.

### 2. Seated Cable Row — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Pause and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end range for a full second.
- **Do:** Keep your torso still — the movement comes from your arms and back, not rocking.
- **Don't:** Round your lower back to get extra range of motion.

### 3. Straight-Arm Pulldown — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** This isolates the lats — keep arms almost fully straight (soft bend) the whole time.
- **Do:** Focus on pulling with your lats, not your triceps.
- **Don't:** Bend your elbows to turn it into a tricep pushdown — that defeats the purpose.

### 4. Barbell or EZ-Bar Curl — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Keep elbows locked at your sides — if they swing forward, drop the weight.
- **Do:** Control the negative (lowering) portion for 2-3 seconds.
- **Don't:** Swing your back or use momentum ("cheat curls") — beginners do this most and it kills bicep growth.

### 5. Hammer Curl — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** This also builds forearm size — squeeze your grip hard throughout.
- **Do:** Keep wrists neutral (thumbs up) the whole rep.
- **Don't:** Let your shoulders shrug up to help lift the weight.

## DAY 3 – Wednesday: Shoulders & Abs

### 1. Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 4 sets x 8-10

- **Pro tip:** Keep a slight arch in your back and don't lock elbows out hard at the top — keep tension on the delts.
- **Do:** Lower until elbows are roughly at ear level.
- **Don't:** Flare the dumbbells too wide at the bottom — this stresses the front of the shoulder.

### 2. Lateral Raise — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Lead with your elbows, not your hands, and tilt your pinky slightly up at the top.
- **Do:** Use lighter weight than you think — strict form here builds way more shoulder width than heavy swinging.
- **Don't:** Shrug your traps to lift the weight — this is the #1 beginner mistake on this exercise.

### 3. Rear Delt Fly — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Hinge forward at the hips and imagine pulling the weights apart, not up.
- **Do:** Keep a soft bend in the elbows and squeeze shoulder blades together.
- **Don't:** Use momentum by rocking your torso back and forth.

### 4. Front Raise — 2 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Raise to eye level only — going higher just shifts work to the traps.
- **Do:** Alternate arms or go together, controlled tempo either way.
- **Don't:** Swing the weight up using your hips.

### 5. Hanging Leg Raise — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Focus on curling your pelvis up at the top rather than just swinging your legs.
- **Do:** Keep the movement slow and controlled on the way down.
- **Don't:** Use momentum/swinging — it turns this into a hip flexor exercise instead of an ab exercise.

### 6. Cable Crunch — 3 sets x 15

- **Pro tip:** Round your spine (crunch), don't just bend at the hips — keep the movement coming from your abs.
- **Do:** Keep your hips fixed and stationary throughout.
- **Don't:** Pull with your arms — your arms are just an anchor, your abs do the work.

## DAY 4 – Thursday: Chest & Triceps (Variation)

### 1. Incline Barbell Press — 4 sets x 8-10

- **Pro tip:** Grip slightly narrower than flat bench to keep tension on the upper chest.
- **Do:** Bar path should travel to your upper chest, not your neck.
- **Don't:** Set the incline too steep — it turns this into a shoulder press.

### 2. Flat Dumbbell Press — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Dumbbells allow a deeper stretch than a barbell — lower until you feel it in your chest, not your shoulder joint.
- **Do:** Rotate palms slightly inward at the top of the press.
- **Don't:** Let the dumbbells clank together and bounce — control the tempo instead.

### 3. Dips (chest-focused) — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Lean your torso forward and let elbows flare slightly to bias the chest over triceps.
- **Do:** Stop when your shoulders reach elbow height — going deeper strains the shoulder.
- **Don't:** Drop straight down with an upright torso — that shifts the work entirely to triceps and stresses the shoulder.

### 4. Skull Crushers (EZ-bar) — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Lower the bar toward your forehead/behind your head slightly, not straight down to your chest, to keep tension on the triceps.
- **Do:** Keep your upper arms perpendicular to the floor throughout.
- **Don't:** Flare your elbows out — keep them tucked to protect your elbow joints.

### 5. Rope Pushdown — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Same cues as Day 1 — this is your "finisher," so focus on the squeeze, not the weight.
- **Do:** Full lockout with a 1-sec squeeze.
- **Don't:** Use back or body English to move the weight.

## DAY 5 – Friday: LEG DAY

### 1. Barbell Back Squat — 4 sets x 8-10

- **Pro tip:** Brace your core like you're about to get punched in the stomach before descending — this protects your spine.
- **Do:** Sit back and down, keeping your chest up and knees tracking over your toes.
- **Don't:** Let your knees cave inward or lift your heels off the floor — both are signs the weight is too heavy for your current control.

### 2. Leg Press — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Place feet slightly higher on the platform to bias glutes and hamstrings more.
- **Do:** Lower until knees reach about 90°, keeping lower back flat against the pad.
- **Don't:** Let your lower back round at the bottom (this happens from going too deep or too heavy) — it's a common cause of lower back strain.

### 3. Romanian Deadlift — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Push your hips back, not down — this isn't a squat, it's a hip hinge.
- **Do:** Keep the bar close to your legs throughout, feeling a stretch in your hamstrings.
- **Don't:** Round your lower back to reach further down — stop the descent once you feel your hamstrings fully stretched.

### 4. Leg Curl (lying or seated) — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Point your toes toward your shins (dorsiflex) to isolate the hamstring better.
- **Do:** Control the negative — don't let the weight snap back.
- **Don't:** Arch your lower back off the pad to generate momentum.

### 5. Leg Extension — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Squeeze and pause for 1 sec at the top of each rep for max quad contraction.
- **Do:** Keep your back flat against the pad the whole set.
- **Don't:** Use a fast, kicking motion — control the weight both up and down.

### 6. Standing Calf Raise — 4 sets x 15-20

- **Pro tip:** Pause at the very bottom (full stretch) for a second before pushing up — most beginners rush this and lose half the benefit.
- **Do:** Rise as high onto your toes as possible and squeeze.
- **Don't:** Use tiny, bouncy partial reps — full range of motion matters more than weight here.

## DAY 6 – Saturday: Back & Biceps (Variation) + Abs

### 1. Lat Pulldown or Assisted Pull-Up — 3 sets x 8-10

- **Pro tip:** If doing pull-ups, focus on pulling your chest to the bar rather than just your chin over it.
- **Do:** Full dead-hang at the bottom for full lat stretch.
- **Don't:** Use a kipping/swinging motion to cheat reps.

### 2. Chest-Supported Row (machine or incline bench + dumbbells) — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Chest support removes momentum from the equation — use this to really feel your back working.
- **Do:** Drive elbows back and squeeze shoulder blades together at the top.
- **Don't:** Let your head jut forward to try to get extra range — keep neck neutral.

### 3. Single-Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** Pull the elbow toward your hip, not straight up — this hits the lats better than a high pull.
- **Do:** Keep your torso parallel to the floor and stable.
- **Don't:** Twist your torso to help lift the weight — that's momentum, not back strength.

### 4. Incline Dumbbell Curl — 3 sets x 10-12

- **Pro tip:** The incline position stretches the bicep more at the bottom — a great size-builder.
- **Do:** Let your arms hang straight down before curling.
- **Don't:** Let your elbows drift forward as the weight gets heavy — this is the same fault as standing curls.

### 5. Preacher Curl — 3 sets x 12-15

- **Pro tip:** Don't fully lock out your elbows at the bottom — keep slight tension to protect the joint.
- **Do:** Control the eccentric (lowering) portion fully.
- **Don't:** Use a full range so fast that your elbows "bounce" at the bottom — this stresses the tendon.

### 6. Plank — 3 sets x 45 sec

- **Pro tip:** Squeeze your glutes and brace your abs like you're about to be poked in the stomach.
- **Do:** Keep a straight line from head to heels.
- **Don't:** Let your hips sag or pike up — both reduce how much your core actually works.

---

## Sunday – Rest Day

Full recovery day. Light walking or stretching is fine, but no lifting. This is when your muscles actually grow — don't skip it even if you feel fine.

## Quick Notes on Your Tuesday Session

You mentioned doing 3 bicep exercises + 2 back exercises (3 sets each) with no plan. That's actually decent volume for those muscles — this plan channels that same effort into **Day 2 and Day 6**, so you're training back/biceps twice a week instead of randomly, with variety between sessions to avoid staleness.

## A note on your leg frequency

You asked for legs only on Friday, so that's what this plan has. Just know legs are the largest muscle group in your body — training them only once a week is the minimum effective dose, not the optimal one. If a few weeks in you want faster "lean" results, adding one light leg accessory session (e.g., a Tuesday or Saturday add-on of leg extensions + calf raises) would help without needing a second full leg day.
