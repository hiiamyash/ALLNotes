#### Question text

After the raid, your team salvaged an abandoned piece of broadcast software from the syndicate’s internal server. This was once their primary tool for mass-distributing orders, but it was discarded after a botched update. Forensic analysis reveals that during the final update, the developer misplaced the key generation logic. Because of this structural error, the app can no longer generate the correct keys to encrypt new messages, which is why it was abandoned.

While the "Encryptor" tool is nowhere to be found, you have successfully recovered the "Decryptor" module. However, the Decryptor is useless as long as the corrupted broadcast app continues to output "broken" keys. To recover the "Hidden Ruth"—the final message broadcasted before the software was trashed—you must reverse-engineer the compiled binary, locate the misplaced generation points in the code, and patch the logic so the keys align with the Decryptor’s requirements.

Only by "fixing" this ghost of a program can you force it to reproduce the last broadcasted key and unlock the truth.
